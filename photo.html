Pull a record off the middle shelf (drag it down) · drop it on the turntable

Load a playlist

  • Grab a spine on the middle shelf and drag down. It slides out towards you and its title comes up.
  • Keep pulling until it's free, then carry it to the blue turntable on the coffee table.
  • Click the record on the turntable to lift or drop the needle. Drag it off to stop.
  • Click a spine to peek at it, click again to push it back. Double-click plays it straight away.

Source

Playback runs through SoundCloud's player. Private playlists and tracks with embedding turned off won't load.
