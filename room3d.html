Dusting the shelves…
Pull a record from the shelf (drag it down towards you) · drop it on the turntable

Load a playlist

  • Grab a spine on the eye-level shelf and drag down to pull it out. The title comes up as it slides.
  • Keep pulling until it's free, then carry it to the turntable on the coffee table.
  • Click the tonearm to lift the needle, and again to drop it. Drag the record off to stop.
  • Drag empty space to look around, scroll or pinch to move closer.

Source

Playback runs through SoundCloud's player. Private playlists and tracks with embedding turned off won't load. The room is modelled in Blender.
