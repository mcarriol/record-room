Load a playlist

  • Grab a spine on the middle shelf above the TV and drag down. It slides out towards you and its title comes up.
  • Keep pulling until it's free, then carry it to the big blue turntable on the coffee table.
  • Drag the tonearm onto the record to start it. The song plays from the beginning. Lift the arm off to stop.
  • Drag the record off the turntable to stop and put it away.
  • Click a spine to peek at it, click again to push it back. Double-click plays it straight away.

Source

Playback runs through SoundCloud's player. Private playlists and tracks with embedding turned off won't load. The room is modelled and rendered in Blender.
