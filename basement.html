WARMING UP THE TUBES…
Drag a record from the coffee table onto the turntable · drag the room to look around

Load a playlist

  • Drag a sleeve from the coffee table onto the turntable to play it.
  • Click the tonearm to lift the needle, and again to drop it.
  • Drag the record off the platter to stop it.
  • Drag empty space to look around, scroll or pinch to move closer. The lamp and the blinds click.

Source

Playback runs through SoundCloud's player. Private playlists and tracks with embedding turned off won't load. The 3D room was modelled in Blender.
